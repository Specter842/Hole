Senior Backend Engineer, Payments Platform
Meridian Systems - Remote (US)

About the role
We are building the payments platform that powers checkout for thousands of
merchants. You will own backend services end to end, from design through
on-call.

What you'll do
- Design and build high-throughput Python services handling payment
  authorization, capture, and refunds
- Improve the reliability and latency of the checkout path
- Work with PostgreSQL at scale, including schema design and query tuning
- Build event-driven integrations with third-party payment processors
- Partner with infrastructure on Kubernetes deployments and CI/CD pipelines
- Mentor engineers and raise the bar on code review

Requirements
- 5+ years building backend services in Python
- Strong API design experience, ideally REST and event-driven systems
- Production experience with PostgreSQL and Redis
- Comfortable with Docker, Kubernetes, and modern CI/CD
- Experience with idempotency, retries, and distributed system failure modes
- Track record of measurable latency or reliability improvements

Nice to have
- Payments or fintech domain experience
- Exposure to PCI DSS compliance
- Experience with Go
- Familiarity with Terraform and AWS

Benefits
Competitive salary, equity, medical and dental, unlimited PTO. Meridian Systems
is an equal opportunity employer.
